# Report safely

Choose the appropriate [issue template](https://github.com/Robby19882025/herald-windows-support/issues/new/choose). Posting requires a GitHub account. Everything posted in these issues is public.

For a technical issue, include only the version, a short description, expected and actual behavior, and synthetic steps that reproduce it. Do not attach complete logs, configuration files or conversation archives.

For inappropriate AI output, describe the category of concern and use a safe paraphrase or invented reproduction. Do not publish illegal material, identifying information, private conversation excerpts or harmful operational details.

For privacy requests or confidential support, email [dragonluckyorigin@gmail.com](mailto:dragonluckyorigin@gmail.com). No GitHub account is required. State the type of request (information, correction or removal) and include only necessary details. Do not post identity documents, addresses or information needed for private identity verification in an issue. Ordinary email is not a promised end-to-end encrypted channel; agree on a suitable method before sending unusually sensitive material.

For a potential vulnerability, do not publish exploit instructions or credentials. Send a minimal report by email to the same publisher address. If further sensitive material is necessary, agree on a suitable transmission method first.

If a credential was accidentally exposed, revoke it through its provider and request removal of the public content. Deleting a post does not revoke a credential or erase copies held by others.

## How the publisher handles reports

The publisher reviews submitted reports, requests only necessary additional information, and removes or redacts unsuitable content within the repository where appropriate. Reports can lead to documentation corrections or a separately reviewed product change. No guaranteed response time is promised.

Closing an issue does not delete it. When a request is completed or deletion is requested, the publisher reviews which personal data remains necessary and removes or anonymizes unnecessary details within their control. There is no automatic fixed deletion schedule. Nonpersonal technical documentation may remain. Confidential email or postal requests are not published as issues. Removal of third-party copies or all provider backups cannot be guaranteed. See the [privacy notice](https://robby19882025.github.io/herald-windows-support/privacy/).
